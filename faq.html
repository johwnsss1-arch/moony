Questions

Short answers about safety, installing and what Moony does to your PC.

Is Moony safe?

Yes. The source code is public on GitHub, and every installer's SHA-256 is listed on the download page so you can check it on VirusTotal. You sign in on Roblox's own login page, so Moony never sees your password. Only download Moony from this site or the GitHub releases page.

Can I get banned for using Moony?

No. Moony isn't a cheat or an exploit. It starts the official Roblox player the normal way and uses Roblox's public website features, like the server list and your friends list.

Does it cost anything?

No. Moony is free, with no ads and no paid features.

Windows says it protected my PC. What do I do?

Windows SmartScreen warns about apps it hasn't seen many people install yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file's SHA-256 against the one on the download page first.

What does setup install?

Moony itself, the Microsoft WebView2 runtime if your PC doesn't already have it (Moony needs it to show its window), and, if you leave that option on, the latest Roblox player so your first game starts right away. It also adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an entry in Apps & features.

Does Moony replace the Roblox player?

No. Moony downloads and runs the official Roblox player. It adds things around it: picking servers, following friends, the HUD and so on.

Does Boost mode change my PC's settings?

Only while you play. It sets Roblox to high CPU priority and switches Windows to the High performance power plan, then puts your previous power plan back when you leave the game or close Moony.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings → Apps, find Moony and press Uninstall. You can choose to keep or delete your accounts and settings. Roblox links go back to opening the regular Roblox player.

Is Moony made by Roblox?

No. Moony is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.

Where do I report a bug or ask for help?

Ask in the Moony Discord, open an issue on GitHub, or use Send feedback in the app's title bar.
