Unlock Roblox FPS and cut lag

Roblox caps your frame rate at 60 by default. Here's how to raise the cap and get smoother games on Windows, without installing anything sketchy.

Raise the frame rate cap

In Moony, open Settings → Roblox and drag Frame rate cap up to 120, 144, 240 or 360 FPS to match your monitor. Restart Roblox and the new cap applies to every game. Set it back to Default to undo it.

Turn on Boost mode

Boost mode gives Roblox high CPU priority and switches Windows to the High performance power plan while you play. When you leave the game, your old power plan comes back. Turn it on from the Performance page.

Join a closer server

A lot of what feels like low FPS is actually ping. Fast join picks the open server with the lowest ping, so your character stops rubber-banding. See how to pick a Roblox server.

Is it safe?

Yes. Moony only changes Roblox's own settings files and Windows power plans, never the game's memory, so it isn't an exploit and won't get you banned. The whole app is open source.
