Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Moony is a free, open-source Roblox launcher for Windows. This page explains what data it handles. The full source code is on GitHub.

Your Roblox account

You sign in on Roblox's own login page; Moony never sees your password. The session cookie Roblox gives back is encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) and stored only on your PC. It is sent only to Roblox's own servers, to do what you ask (launch games, load friends, and so on). We never receive it.

What stays on your PC

Settings, themes, favorites, playtime stats and game history are saved locally on your computer and are never uploaded.

What is sent to us

  • Feedback. If you use the feedback form, your message (and the answer to "how did you find Moony", if you give one) is sent to our Discord server. Don't include personal information you don't want shared.
  • Update checks. Moony checks GitHub for new versions. GitHub sees a normal web request (your IP address), like visiting any website.

Moony has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we don't sell or share data.

Third parties

Moony talks to Roblox, GitHub, Discord (only if you turn on Discord status) and moonylauncher.xyz (for themes, cursors and presets). Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Moony, or uninstall it, to remove your saved login. Uninstalling removes the rest of Moony's local data. To delete feedback you sent, ask us in our Discord.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub or ask in our Discord.
