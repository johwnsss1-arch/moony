Install in a minute

  1. Run Moony-Setup.exeIf Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for new apps it hasn't seen much yet.
  2. Pick your optionsChoose where to install, your shortcuts, and whether to download Roblox now so your first game starts right away.
  3. Press InstallSetup adds the Microsoft WebView2 runtime if your PC doesn't have it, then copies Moony and adds it to Apps & features.
  4. Open Moony and sign inYou sign in on Roblox's own login page. Then pick a game.

Uninstalling

Remove Moony from Settings → Apps like any other app. The uninstaller asks whether to keep your accounts and settings, and gives Roblox links back to the normal Roblox player.
